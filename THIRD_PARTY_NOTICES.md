# Attribution and rights

The supplied collection contains student exercises, preliminary notes, instructor solutions, copied utilities, assignment documents and externally sourced datasets. Their presence in a local folder does not grant redistribution rights over all materials.

The `JorgePereira_*` exercises are presented as the supplied student's coursework. TP0 notes have no clear individual attribution and are labeled preliminary course material. Instructor solutions bearing another author's name are excluded rather than presented as the portfolio owner's work.

The copied NIfTI helper functions (`load_nii*`, `xform_nii`) are not included or relicensed. Historical TP6 scripts still reference them and require a separately obtained compatible loader. Their exclusion is explicit; this repository is not a distribution of the NIfTI toolbox.

Original photographs, artwork, scans, EEG/instrument recordings, MP3 audio, derived masks, filter objects, videos, PDFs and Word reports are not distributed here. Synthetic examples generated by `examples/run_demo.m` are separate maintenance assets and do not reproduce those inputs.

MATLAB and its toolboxes remain MathWorks products with their own licensing terms. No MATLAB runtime or toolbox implementation is bundled. The CI workflow uses official MATLAB actions; see their linked documentation in the validation report.

No blanket license is assigned to the imported academic material without confirmation from the rights holder. Academic publication does not imply institutional endorsement, a transfer of third-party rights or clinical validation of image-processing exercises.
